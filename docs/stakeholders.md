## Stakeholders

The Study Planner system involves several stakeholders who either directly use the system or are indirectly affected by its operation. Identifying stakeholders ensures that the system addresses real user needs and aligns with institutional objectives.

### 1. Primary Stakeholder – Students (End Users)

Students are the primary users of the Study Planner. They use the system to:

- Load module and coursework data
- Plan study tasks and milestones
- Track study activities
- Monitor deadlines and progress
- Visualise workload using dashboards and Gantt charts

Their main goals are to:
- Improve organisation and time management
- Avoid missed deadlines
- Understand progress toward coursework completion
- Balance workload across multiple modules

The system is designed primarily around the needs, behaviour, and constraints of this group.

---

### 2. Client – School of Computing Sciences (UEA)

The School of Computing Sciences acts as the commissioning body for the system. The School’s interests include:

- Supporting student academic performance
- Encouraging structured study planning
- Reducing last-minute deadline pressure
- Potentially integrating the system with existing university services

The client’s objectives influence system requirements such as file-based module data loading and potential future integration with the university hub.

---

### 3. Module Organisers and Academic Staff

Module organisers are indirect stakeholders because:

- They provide coursework and deadline information.
- Deadline changes or extensions must be supported by the system.
- Their updates affect how students use the Study Planner.

The system must therefore support flexible deadline updates and accurate representation of assessment events.

---

### 4. University IT Services / Hub System

University IT services are external stakeholders because:

- The Study Planner loads module data provided by the university hub.
- Future integration may require API connectivity.
- Data format decisions impact compatibility and maintainability.

Although not direct users, their systems influence technical constraints and integration decisions.

---

### 5. Future Stakeholders – Other Schools within UEA

While initially developed for the School of Computing Sciences, the system may later support students from other schools. This future stakeholder group influences:

- The need for adaptable module structures
- Scalability of the data model
- Generalisation of coursework and assessment types

---

## Stakeholder Summary

The Study Planner is primarily student-focused, but its design must also consider institutional goals, academic workflows, and potential future integration with university systems. Balancing these stakeholder needs ensures the system is both practically useful and technically extensible.
