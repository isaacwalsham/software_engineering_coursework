# Personas

## Persona 1: Steve Beaton

- **Age:** 20  
- **Background:**  
  Second-year UEA Computer Science student taking multiple programming-heavy modules with overlapping coursework deadlines. Regularly uses online tools but struggles with long-term planning.

- **Goals:**  
  - Break large coursework assignments into manageable tasks  
  - Track progress across multiple modules in one place  
  - Avoid missing deadlines or leaving work until the last minute  

- **Frustrations:**  
  - Deadlines spread across different systems and documents  
  - Difficulty understanding how much work is realistically left  
  - Generic to-do lists that do not show task dependencies  

- **Typical scenario of use:**  
  At the start of the semester, Steve imports module and coursework data, creates study tasks for each assignment, and checks the dashboard weekly to monitor upcoming deadlines and overall progress.

## Persona 2: Michelle Lush

- **Age:** 22  
- **Background:**  
  Final-year UEA student balancing dissertation work, taught modules, and part-time employment. Has limited study time and needs to prioritise effectively.

- **Goals:**  
  - Plan study time efficiently around work commitments  
  - Visualise dependencies between tasks and milestones  
  - Track time spent studying to ensure consistent progress  

- **Frustrations:**  
  - Poor visibility of workload across different modules  
  - Forgetting smaller milestones that contribute to major deadlines  
  - Difficulty judging whether she is “on track”  

- **Typical scenario of use:**  
  Michelle logs study activities after each session, uses the Gantt chart to plan work around her job, and relies on the dashboard to identify at-risk deadlines early.

---

## Persona 3: Ben Williams

- **Age:** 19  
- **Background:**  
  First-year UEA Computer Science student adjusting to independent learning for the first time. Finds it difficult to organise study tasks without external structure.

- **Goals:**  
  - Understand what needs to be done and by when  
  - Build effective study habits early in the semester  
  - Gain confidence in managing multiple modules  

- **Frustrations:**  
  - Feeling overwhelmed by the volume of coursework  
  - Uncertainty about how much work is sufficient  
  - Lack of clear feedback from basic planning tools  

- **Typical scenario of use:**  
  Ben uses the Study Planner to follow a structured task list generated from coursework deadlines and checks progress indicators regularly to stay motivated and on schedule.
