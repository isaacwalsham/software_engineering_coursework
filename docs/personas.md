# 1.5 Personas

## Persona 1: Steve Beaton

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 20 |
| **Background** | Second-year UEA Computer Science student taking multiple programming-heavy modules with overlapping coursework deadlines. Regularly uses online tools but struggles with long-term planning. |
| **Goals** | Break large coursework assignments into manageable tasks; track progress across multiple modules in one place; avoid missing deadlines or leaving work until the last minute |
| **Frustrations** | Deadlines spread across different systems and documents; difficulty understanding how much work is realistically left; generic to-do lists that do not show task dependencies |
| **Typical Scenario** | At the start of the semester, Steve imports module and coursework data, creates study tasks for each assignment, and checks the dashboard weekly to monitor upcoming deadlines and overall progress |

---

## Persona 2: Michelle Lush

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 22 |
| **Background** | Final-year UEA student balancing dissertation work, taught modules, and part-time employment. Has limited study time and needs to prioritise effectively. |
| **Goals** | Plan study time efficiently around work commitments; visualise dependencies between tasks and milestones; track time spent studying to ensure consistent progress |
| **Frustrations** | Poor visibility of workload across different modules; forgetting smaller milestones that contribute to major deadlines; difficulty judging whether she is on track |
| **Typical Scenario** | Michelle logs study activities after each session, uses the Gantt chart to plan work around her job, and relies on the dashboard to identify at-risk deadlines early |

---

## Persona 3: Ben Williams

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 19 |
| **Background** | First-year UEA Computer Science student adjusting to independent learning for the first time. Finds it difficult to organise study tasks without external structure. |
| **Goals** | Understand what needs to be done and by when; build effective study habits early in the semester; gain confidence in managing multiple modules |
| **Frustrations** | Feeling overwhelmed by the volume of coursework; uncertainty about how much work is sufficient; lack of clear feedback from basic planning tools |
| **Typical Scenario** | Ben uses the Study Planner to follow a structured task list generated from coursework deadlines and checks progress indicators regularly to stay motivated and on schedule |
